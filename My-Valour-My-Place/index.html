My Valour, My Place!

Task 2 ★ Express Your Place

Write a poem, prose or song about a place in school or near school. Then draw and colour your poster on paper, take a clear photograph and upload it to your class Padlet!

📍 Step 1: Find Your Special Place

Choose a place in Valour Primary School or nearby that brings back a memory or makes you feel something special.

👀 Step 2: Gather Your Ideas

Imagine you are standing in this place. What do you see, hear, smell or feel? Think about a happy or meaningful memory.

✍️ Step 3: Express Your Place

Choose one form of writing. You can write a poem, prose (a descriptive paragraph) or song lyrics.

🌟 Step 4: Check Your Writing

Read your work aloud. Tick the boxes only after checking each success criterion.

✨ Can you replace vague words such as “nice” and “good” with words that help readers see or feel the place?

🎨 Step 5: Draw Your Poster and Upload to Padlet

Your writing is now ready to become a hand-drawn poster. You do not need to design anything on this website.

🖍️ Draw on paper

1. Use a sheet of paper (for example, A4).
2. Write your creative title clearly.
3. Copy your finished poem, prose or song neatly.
4. Draw the chosen place and add colours.
5. Make sure the writing is readable.

📸 Upload to Padlet

1. Write your name and class neatly on your poster.
2. Take a clear photograph showing your entire completed poster.
3. Open your class Padlet using the link shared by your English teacher.
4. Click + to create a new post and enter your poster title.
5. Attach the photo, check it is clear, then click Publish.
