Dogs with Jobs: Main Ideas & Supporting Details
Primary 5 • Read → Identify → Organise → Write

🎯 Learning Goal

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to identify a main idea, find supporting details, and then use them to plan and write a short information paragraph about a service dog.

Remember: A supporting detail must link back to the main idea. It explains the main idea, gives a specific example, or adds key facts.
Main Idea

What is this part mostly about?

The main idea is the most important point of a paragraph or section. It tells us the overall topic being discussed.

Supporting Details

How do I know?

Supporting details explain, describe, give examples, or provide interesting facts that directly support the main idea.

🔎 A Useful Reading Routine

Follow these steps whenever you read a text to find key information:

1

Skim the Gist

Skim the title, headings, sub-headings, or the first sentence of a paragraph to understand the general topic.

2

Ask the Big Question

Ask yourself: "What is this paragraph mainly telling me?" Look for a sentence that sums it up.

3

Scan for Evidence

Scan the rest of the text for specific facts, explanations, or examples that add details about that main idea.

4

Check the Connection

Verify: "Does every supporting detail connect back to the main idea?" If a detail doesn't, it might belong elsewhere.

Teacher Tip: Skimming headings helps readers quickly grasp the primary topic, while scanning helps target specific facts without reading every word.

Dogs make great pets. They are loyal and enjoy being around people. They can help reduce loneliness and stress. Their presence can also make people feel happier.
Question A: Which sentence gives the main idea?
Question B: Why are the other sentences supporting details?

💡 Pattern to Notice

Look at how the main idea is supported by multiple detailed facts in a paragraph structure:

Main Idea:

Dogs make great pets.

Supporting Details:
  • Dogs are loyal and enjoy human company.
  • They help decrease feelings of loneliness.
  • They lower stress levels.
  • They lift people's overall mood.
